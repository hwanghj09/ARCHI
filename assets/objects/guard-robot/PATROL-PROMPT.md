## Animation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production animation sprite atlas for ARCHI, a grounded Korean office mystery pixel-art side-scrolling game. Draw ONE consistent small civilian autonomous security robot: 120-140cm tall relative to a 175cm adult, smooth ivory-white and graphite corporate equipment housing, short squared sensor head on a swivel neck, front-facing camera and distance sensor, tiny calm BLUE status strips around head and torso, narrow white body, broad low black wheeled chassis, visible rubber drive wheel with four small hub spokes and a small stabilizer caster. Friendly utilitarian hotel/service robot; no arms, legs, weapons or mascot face. Subtle worn panel seams and realistic materials, crisp controlled pixel clusters in muted low-light game palette, not glossy 3D. Strict orthographic SIDE VIEW, robot front points RIGHT in every cell, no isometric perspective. Deliver 2048x1024 RGBA atlas: EXACTLY 4 columns by 2 rows of equal 512x512 cells, NO separators, NO labels. Same robot, fixed torso/chassis position and scale in all eight cells. Wheels bottom at y=474 in EACH cell, chassis horizontal center x=256, overall height roughly 384px, generous transparent margins. Frame order row-major: 0 rolling forward wheel hub spokes 0 degrees; 1 spokes 45 degrees; 2 spokes 90 degrees; 3 spokes 135 degrees, blue body LEDs gently breathe, torso stays steady with NO walking bob because robot has wheels. Frame4 stopped inspecting ahead, sensor head slightly lifted; frame5 stopped swiveling sensor head slightly toward the viewer, blue LEDs; frame6 stopped swiveling sensor head back toward forward-right path, blue LEDs; frame7 exact same pose as frame0 but power OFF, all LEDs unlit, lens still black, opaque ivory shell still intact. Side wheel spokes must visibly change while outer wheel radius, base, body and grounding stay fixed. Every pose complete and wholly inside its own cell. No humans, shadows, environment, floor plane, captions, text, digits, watermarks, fake checkerboard or colored background. REAL transparent alpha outside robot, and opaque solid robot surfaces inside. All 8 cells equally spaced.
